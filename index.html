    Welcome

    A digital companion to A Greek and Arabic Lexicon (GALex), Volumes 1 (Alif) & 2 (Bāʾ), edited by Rüdiger Arnzen, Gerhard Endress and Dimitri Gutas (Brill, 2020 & 2021).

    Search by Arabic, transliteration, or Greek. Each entry now displays its Belegstellen — discrete evidence units linking a Greek passage, its Arabic rendering, and the precise source citation. Click any Greek word to jump to its glossary entry; click a source (e.g. Aristotle, De Caelo) to see every belegstelle from that work.

    Use the Browse tab to explore by Arabic root or by ancient author.
